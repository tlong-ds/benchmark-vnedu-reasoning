All sensitivity subsets below use the same whole-triplet bootstrap (10,000 replicates; seed 20261013) as the principal estimate ($\cde=10.9$ points, [7.3, 14.8]; 162 items).

\emph{Human-screened.} Requiring both raters to judge the corruption detectable and answer-relevant and both to confirm placebo meaning preservation selects 117 items (51 triplets, 25 complete) with $\cde=12.1$ points (95\% interval: [8.2, 16.6]). This screen already excludes the one revised stimulus with unresolved propagation uniqueness.

\emph{Strict intersection.} Further restricting to protocol-complete items whose knowledge probes all pass leaves 88 items (49 triplets) with $\cde=13.4$ points ([8.3, 19.0]); restricting to the 13 triplets complete under all criteria (39 items) gives $\cde=10.9$ points ([5.6, 16.5]).

\emph{Protocol and stimulus exclusions.} The 140 protocol-complete items give $\cde=10.4$ points ([6.5, 14.9]). Excluding only the unresolved stimulus from the broad corpus leaves 161 items with $\cde=10.9$ points ([7.4, 14.8]); this exclusion concerns stimulus validity, not individual draws.

All subsets reproduce a positive corrupted-versus-placebo effect with intervals excluding zero. Raters never saw model outputs, so these screens use no outcome information.
