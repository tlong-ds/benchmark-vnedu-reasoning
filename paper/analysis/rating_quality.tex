Human agreement uses paired corrupted-prefix and placebo ratings across all 162 executed items, evaluated independently by two raters under blinded evaluation protocols. Raters saw only the rating sheets, never model outputs; the ratings did not determine inclusion in the 162-item primary analysis or change any outcome label.

Quadratic-weighted detectability $\kappa_w$ is 0.282 (62 exact matches and 35 differences of at least two points). For binary answer relevance across 160 valid pairs (2 pairs contain missing/not-applicable relevance where detectability was rated zero), raw observed agreement is 96.88\% (155/160: 153 two-positive, 2 two-negative, and 5 conflicting pairs). Due to the high-prevalence paradox \citep{feinstein1990high}, where 97.2\% of marginal ratings are positive, Cohen's $\kappa$ is suppressed to 0.429; prevalence-adjusted measures confirm high concordance (PABAK $= \RelevancePABAK$, Gwet's $\text{AC}_1 = \RelevanceGwet$).

Placebo reviews have 162 paired human ratings ($\kappa=0.568$, observed agreement 87.04\%): 122 two-positive, 19 two-negative, and 21 conflicting pairs. A separate pool of 29 clean catch stimuli has 58 judgments and 3 false alarms (5.2\%). Catches are excluded from corruption agreement.
