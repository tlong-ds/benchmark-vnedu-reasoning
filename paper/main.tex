\begin{abstract}
Large language models increasingly assist Vietnamese learners, yet a fluent displayed rationale need not determine the final answer. We introduce a Vietnamese benchmark measuring behavioural dependence on displayed reasoning across computational, theoretical, and history/\allowbreak literature problem types. The candidate pool contains 72 matched triplets (216 items) across four education levels. For DeepSeek-R1-0528 under an unfinished-\textnormal{\textless{}think\textgreater{}} continuation protocol, we compare continuation from clean, placebo-edited, and corrupted step-3 prefixes. Across 162 executed items (54 triplets; 9,720 generations), corrupted step-3 reasoning increases error propagation by \CDEpp\ percentage points (95\% bootstrap CI [\CDECILow, \CDECIHigh], excluding zero), while placebo rewriting induces 0.0\% propagation. In exploratory regression, higher answer-only accuracy is associated with lower propagation (pooled cluster-robust $\beta_q = -20.7$\,pp per unit $q_i$, $p = 0.033$, uncorrected); slopes by problem type are not statistically distinguishable (omnibus $p = 0.075$). Auditing continuations across all 2,846 corrupted gold returns indicates that keyword-based silent override dominates (\GoldSilentOverridePct\% by keyword audit; $\approx 66\%$--$74\%$ after calibration), with explicit repair $\approx 26\%$--$34\%$. Failing the factual-prerequisite probes was not associated with more propagation (10.5\% vs.\ 13.4\%), although items needing external facts propagated more often than self-contained ones (descriptive, confounded with problem type). Data and code are available at {\def\UrlBreaks{\do\/}\def\UrlBigBreaks{\do\/}\url{https://github.com/tlong-ds/benchmark-vnedu-reasoning}}.
\end{abstract}
